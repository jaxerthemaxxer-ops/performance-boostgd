# Performance Boost

Raises your framerate in levels by switching off costly visual effects.

## Features

- Disable player particles (ground, trailing, land, dash, burst, click)
- Disable regular and wave trails
- Ignore camera shake
- Hide the level background
- Free unused textures when the main menu loads
- Live FPS overlay with average and worst frame time, in any corner

Every option can be toggled in the mod settings and takes effect immediately.
